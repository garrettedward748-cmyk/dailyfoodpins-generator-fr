FIELD REQUIREMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

title
- MUST contain a colon. The dish's own name, then a colon, then a plain
  description of what it is, in French.
- Pattern: « Msemen : crêpes feuilletées marocaines », « Chorba frik : soupe
  algérienne à l'agneau et au freekeh », « Tacos façon tarte aux pommes : dessert croustillant ». Use a plain ASCII colon, and put
  a space before it only if the French pattern calls for it.
- Keep the local name first when the dish has one. Do not translate it away.
- NEVER end the title with the word « Recette ». A title without a colon, or one
  ending in « Recette », is wrong: put the colon form here, not in the headline.
- Under 65 characters.

slug
- The title, lowercase, hyphens, no colon, no filler words. ASCII only: drop the accents (é becomes e, ç becomes c, œ becomes oe).

headline
- One line under the title. A plain sentence fragment, 8 to 14 words, saying
  what makes the dish work or what the result is like.
- MUST NOT contain a colon, and must not repeat the title or restate its
  description. If the title is « Msemen : crêpes feuilletées marocaines », the
  headline is something like « Pliée et étirée finement pour cuire en
  couches plutôt qu'en une seule galette ».
- No full stop at the end.

title_tag
- The browser tab title. Under 60 characters. Carries the focus keyword.

meta_desc
- One or two sentences, 140 to 155 characters, describing the dish and the
  result. Carries the focus keyword once, naturally.

focus_keyword
- The single search phrase this page targets. Usually the SEO keyword above,
  lowercase.

{{structureIntro}}

- Weave the focus keyword into the first paragraph once, where it reads
