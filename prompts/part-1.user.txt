═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
FIELD REQUIREMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

title
- MUST contain a colon. The dish's own name, then a colon, then a plain
  description of what it is.
- Pattern: "Msemen: Moroccan Square Flatbread", "Chorba Frik: Algerian Lamb
  and Freekeh Soup", "Apple Pie Cheesecake Tacos: Sweet Tortilla Dessert".
- Keep the local name first when the dish has one. Do not translate it away.
- NEVER end the title with the word "Recipe". A title without a colon, or one
  ending in "Recipe", is wrong: put the colon form here, not in the headline.
- Under 65 characters.

slug
- The title, lowercase, hyphens, no colon, no filler words. ASCII only.

headline
- One line under the title. A plain sentence fragment, 8 to 14 words, saying
  what makes the dish work or what the result is like.
- MUST NOT contain a colon, and must not repeat the title or restate its
  description. If the title is "Msemen: Moroccan Square Flatbread", the
  headline is something like "Folded and stretched thin so it cooks in
  layers, not one slab".
- No full stop at the end.

title_tag
- The browser tab title. Under 60 characters. Carries the focus keyword.

meta_desc
- One or two sentences, 140 to 155 characters, describing the dish and the
  result. Carries the focus keyword once, naturally.

focus_keyword
- The single search phrase this page targets. Usually the SEO keyword above,
  lowercase.

intro_html
{{structureIntro}}
- Address the cook as "you". No anecdote. No history lesson. No promise.
- Weave the focus keyword into the first paragraph once, where it reads
  naturally. Never twice.
