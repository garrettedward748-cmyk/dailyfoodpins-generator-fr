═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
FIELD REQUIREMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

servings
- A whole number, from the source. No text, no range.

servings_unit
- What one serving is, in French: « portions », « pièces », « galettes », « bols ».

yield_text
- How much the recipe makes, in words: « Pour 12 galettes ». Leave "" if the
  servings number already says it.

prep_minutes / cook_minutes / rest_minutes
- Whole numbers of minutes: {{times.card}}
- rest_minutes covers resting, proving, marinating and chilling. It is often
  the number that decides the dish — never fold it into prep.
- Do not output a total. It is worked out from these three.

difficulty
- Exactly one of: easy, medium, hard.

ingredients
- An array of objects, ONE PER LINE of the list, in the order they are used.
- Shape: {"amount": "", "unit": "", "item": "", "note": ""}
  - amount: the number only, as written: "2", "1/2", "250".
  - unit: one of {{style.ingredientUnits}}. Leave "" for whole things
    (« 2 oignons » is amount "2", unit "", item "oignons").
  - item: the ingredient, no quantity inside it.
  - note: the preparation, if any: « finement haché », « à température ambiante ».
    Leave "" when there is none.
- 8 to 14 lines; when the source lists more than 14 ingredients, one line
  per source ingredient instead (never drop one). Do NOT group them into sections and do NOT add a "section"
  key: this site lists ingredients as one flat list.
- The same ingredient used in two parts of the dish (lemon juice in the sauce
  and in the marinade, salt in both) gets one line per part, and each of those
  lines says in its note which part it is for: « pour le tzatziki », « pour la
  marinade ». Without it the list reads as a mistake: the same thing twice.

steps
- An array of 6 to 8 objects, in order.
- Shape: {"heading": "", "body": "", "timer_seconds": null}
  - heading: 2 to 5 words, imperative, naming the action.
    « Préparez une pâte souple », « Blanchissez les olives », « Étirez finement ».
  - body: 2 to 4 sentences. Say what to do, what it should look, feel or
    smell like when it is right, and why that matters. Address the cook as
    « vous ». Keep the source's exact times and temperatures, written the way this site
    writes them: {{style.temperatureExample}}.
  - timer_seconds: whole seconds when the step has one unattended wait
    (simmering, resting, baking). null otherwise. 20 minutes is 1200.
- Cover the whole method. Do not stop early and do not merge two techniques
  into one step to save space.

tips_html
- ONE cook's note, as a single <p>, 30 to 60 words.
- The thing that most often goes wrong, and how to tell it is going wrong in
  time to fix it. Not a summary of the steps. Not encouragement.
