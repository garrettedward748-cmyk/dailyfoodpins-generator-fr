═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ONE DISH, STEP BY STEP
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Each photograph is generated on its own, from nothing but its prompt. So
before writing any of them, work out what the food looks like after EVERY
step of the method: its shape, its size, how many pieces, and everything
already on it or in it.

- A method shot shows the food exactly at that point. Everything done in
  the EARLIER steps is still there — the filling, the fold, the crimped
  edge, the crumble, the glaze, the dusting — and nothing from a LATER step
  appears yet. Pastries sprinkled with streusel in step 5 still carry that
  streusel when they are brushed with egg wash in step 6.
- The shape the method makes is the shape in every photograph, the hero and
  the pin included. If the steps fold squares into half-moons, every shot
  shows half-moons, whatever the title calls the dish.
- Write that shape, the filling and every topping already added into each
  prompt in plain words. A prompt that only names the action ("brushing the
  pastries") gets a picture of some other pastry.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CHOOSING THE THREE METHOD SHOTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Number the recipe's steps from 1. Choose the THREE where a cook stops and
wonders whether they have got it right — where the answer is something you
can see:

  - a texture or consistency that has to be reached (a slack dough, a sauce
    that coats the spoon, a batter with bubbles across its face)
  - a shape or seal that decides the result (folded, rolled, sealed, layered)
  - a colour or state to stop at (the browning, the reduction, the set)

Do NOT choose a step whose picture would be a bowl of ingredients, a pan of
water, or the finished dish on a plate — the hero already shows that. Never
choose the serving step.

Return each one's step number. They must be three DIFFERENT numbers, in
ascending order, and each must be a step that exists in the recipe above.

hero
- The finished dish as it is eaten, with one portion torn, cut or lifted to
  show the inside. This is the picture that appears at the top of the

- End it with: Editorial food blog photography, 4:3.

step_a, step_b, step_c
- One photograph of each chosen moment, in step order. Show the state the
  step is about — the thinness, the seal, the colour — close enough to judge
  it by eye. No hands.
- Each must be visibly a different composition from the other two and from
  the hero: change the angle, the distance, or what is in the frame.
- End each with: Editorial food blog photography, 3:2.

pin_title
- The words printed on the Pinterest graphic, written in French. 2 to 6 words,
  French capitalisation (first word and proper nouns only), the dish's name or
  the promise of it: « Msemen feuilleté », « La galette qui se déchire en
  feuilles ». Every accent must be correct.
- No exclamation marks, no « incontournable », no « révolutionnaire », no emoji, no
  hashtags, no site name.

pin
- A vertical photograph of the finished dish, composed taller than the hero
  and from a different angle, with the same surface, props and palette.
- State that the top third of the frame is clean, uncluttered background with

- Do NOT write the title into this prompt; it is added separately.
- End it with: Editorial food blog photography, 2:3.
